# Quality retrospective

**Goal:** learn from a release or an incident without blame, and leave with a small number of actions the team owns.

**Participants:** everyone who worked on the release or the incident, including support.

| Minutes | Activity | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Ground rules and check-in: one word for how the release felt | — |
| 5–20 | What happened? | What, So What, Now What? (step 1) on a timeline |
| 20–35 | Why does it matter? | 1-2-4-All (W³ step 2) |
| 35–45 | Help from the outside | Troika Consulting |
| 45–55 | Now what? | 15% Solutions (W³ step 3) |
| 55–60 | Close | — |

## Activities

1. **What happened? (15 min).** On a timeline from planning to production, each person adds facts only: what was seen, when, by whom. No causes yet. The facilitator reads the timeline back and fills the gaps with the group.
2. **So what? (15 min).** "What patterns do you see? What does this tell us about how we work?" Individual → pairs → fours → all. Focus on conditions (unclear criteria, unstable environment, late test data), not on people.
3. **Troika Consulting (10 min).** In threes, one person describes the challenge they care most about; the other two consult while the first listens with their back turned (or camera off), then the client shares what was useful.
4. **Now what? (10 min).** "What will you do in the next sprint, within your own discretion?" Pick at most three team actions, each with an owner and a check date.

## Ground rules

- We look for causes in the system, not culprits.
- Facts first, interpretations later.
- Every action has an owner and a date, or it is not an action.
- Actions are reviewed at the start of the next retrospective.
